# ReelReady business model: give away the secret, sell the implementation

The kit is free and good enough to stand on its own. People pay Thrive Labs to skip the setup, get the taste right, and never think about it again.

## The ladder

| Step | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Free kit | GitHub repo. Reels end with "comment STYLES" and an automatic DM sends the link. | $0 |
| 2. Done-With-You | We install it, run the questionnaire, tune styles from their reference videos, make their first 3 edits together, then 30 days of support. | $500 list |
| 3. Done-For-You | Telegram concierge. They film, send, get the edit back. We review every export. Monthly style refresh. | two plans below |
| 4. Upsell menu | Website, local SEO, AI integration, automations, infrastructure. Offered only when a real problem shows up. | quoted |

**Done-For-You, two price points** (cafe, salon, contractor):

- **Steady, $297/mo.** Up to 8 edits, review of every export, monthly style refresh. Reasoning: a freelance editor charges $50 to $150 a reel, so 8 reels already cost $400 to $1,200. This is clearly cheaper and still pays for our review time. Fits a cafe or a solo stylist.
- **Busy, $597/mo.** Up to 20 edits, a 30-minute monthly call on what to film next, one small upsell included (for example a Google Business Profile tune-up). Reasoning: a contractor or a multi-chair salon gets value from a single booked job, so $597 is easy to justify and a social agency would charge $1,500+. Fits businesses that post most days.

Both: month to month, cancel any time. Watch usage in the first 3 months before changing prices.

## Founding-client offer

For the first few local businesses: a free Done-With-You setup. In return:

1. Three warm introductions to other business owners.
2. One storytelling reel or post about the experience, in their own words.
3. A review.
4. Permission to use before/after clips.

**In-person script**

"I built a tool that turns a phone clip into a finished reel, and I'm giving it away. I'm setting it up for a few local businesses first, no charge, so I can learn what works. All I'd ask is that you introduce me to three other owners you think it could help, and tell the story of how it went in your own words, whatever you honestly think. If it's not useful, you've lost an hour. Want to try it?"

**Text version**

> Hey, it's [your name]. I built a tool that turns phone clips into finished reels.
> I'm setting it up free for a few local businesses to see what works.
> In return I'd ask for 3 intros and an honest story about how it went.
> Want me to set yours up this week?

## Objections

1. **"I don't have time."** "Setup takes about an hour with me. After that you film and send it."
2. **"AI videos look fake."** "Your own footage and your own voice. Captions are word for word, and nothing gets posted without your okay."
3. **"It's free, so what's the catch?"** "The tool is free. If you want me to run it for you, that's a paid plan. If you don't, keep it."
4. **"My nephew does my videos."** "Keep him. This handles the boring cutting so he can film and plan."
5. **"Is my footage private?"** "Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to us."

## Success in 30 days

- First 3 edits done and posted.
- At least 8 reels posted in the month, roughly 2 a week.
- Under 10 minutes of their time per reel.
- They can name one reel they are proud of.
- The 3 intros, story, and review have happened.

If those hold, the Done-For-You conversation is easy.

## Why free (README footer)

Thrive Labs is an AI and web agency. We built ReelReady for our own clients and decided to share it, because the tool is not the hard part. The hard part is knowing which style fits your business, setting it up properly, and keeping it running week after week. So the kit is free, and you can run it yourself. If you would rather have it set up for you, tuned to your brand, and looked after, that is what we offer. Either way, you get the kit.
