# Sounding like the owner

The script is in their voice or it is slop. Learn the voice from their past reels before writing.

## Read

The 5 newest transcripts under `<main folder>/.reelready/work/*/transcript.json` (or
`draft.words.json`). Read the text, not the timings.

## Note, in your head, before writing

- **Their phrases.** Words they repeat ("honestly", "you guys", "our little cafe"). Reuse one or two.
  Don't overdo a catchphrase.
- **Sentence length.** Short bursts or long flowing lines? Match it.
- **How they open and close.** If they always say "see you next time", the script can end that way.
  Never open with a greeting though: the hook comes first.
- **Their names for things.** If they say "chai" not "tea latte", "the shop" not "the store", use theirs.
- **What they never say.** If there's no hype in their past reels, there's none in the script.

## Prefer their exact lines

A line the owner already said on camera beats anything you write. When a past line fits a beat,
quote it and source it (`your past reel <folder>: "<exact words>"`). The check confirms the words
are really in that transcript, so copy them exactly as transcribed.

## No past reels yet

Fall back to `profile.tone` (default warm, direct, local) and plain words a customer would use.
Tell the owner once that the script won't fully sound like them yet, and to change any line freely.
After their first edited reel, scripts will have their voice to draw on.
