# Hook formulas

A hook is the first on-screen title or the first line of the post caption. It earns the next
3 seconds. Every hook starts from a line the owner said; the formula only shapes it.

## Pick 3 different angles

| Angle | Shape | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| Specific | the most concrete thing said: a number, a time, a named item | "we make 40 loaves before 7" -> "40 loaves before 7 a.m." |
| Curiosity | point at the payoff without giving it away | "the back room is a roaster" -> "What's behind our back room door" |
| Owner's line | the best 4-7 word stretch they said, near verbatim | "every Tuesday we roast right here" -> "Every Tuesday, we roast right here" |
| Before / after | the change they described, both sides | "used to buy beans in, now we roast" -> "We used to buy our beans in" |
| Viewer's question | the question a customer already asks, answered in the reel | they explain oat vs dairy -> "Oat or dairy in a flat white?" |
| Contrast | a belief they said is wrong, flipped | "people think sourdough is sour" -> "Sourdough isn't meant to taste sour" |

Use `profile.reference_style.hook_patterns` as a tie-breaker when it names one of these.
Don't repeat the hook title already in the reel (`plan.md`); offer alternatives to it.

## Rules

- Under 8 words. Count them.
- Digits over spelled-out numbers ("3", not "three"), but only numbers the owner said.
- Lead with a thing, a number or "you" rather than "I" where it reads naturally.
- Curiosity must pay off inside the reel. If the reel doesn't answer it, don't ask it.
- No question that the reel doesn't answer, no exclamation marks, no all caps, no em dashes.

## Never

- Clickbait: "You won't believe...", "Nobody talks about...", "This changes everything",
  "The secret they don't want you to know", "Stop scrolling".
- Ragebait or put-downs: "Your coffee is lying to you", "Chain cafes are ruining...".
- Fake stakes or urgency: "Last chance", "Before it's too late", unless the owner said it.
- Superlatives the owner didn't say: best, #1, only, cheapest, award-winning.
- Name-dropping people, brands or places that aren't in the transcript.
